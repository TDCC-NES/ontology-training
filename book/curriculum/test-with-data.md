## Sentence test

A useful check is to read representative RDF statements as sentences: “B001 is a Bicycle”, “every Bicycle is a Vehicle” and “B001 has component F001”. Follow B001's component link to F001 to find the frame's material. This *sentence test* helps check that the statements express the intended meaning and that the information describes the right thing. You can review the statements yourself or discuss them with colleagues.
